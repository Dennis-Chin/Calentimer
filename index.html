Calentimer — The Hard-Stop Focus Timer & Dynamic Schedule Planner

Calentimer is a free, installable productivity web application combining a precision rotary focus timer with an intelligent daily schedule planner. Set your hard-stop deadline, organize tasks, and let Calentimer dynamically calculate proportional task durations so you always finish your work on time.

Key Features

  • Automatic Time Compression: Tasks automatically shrink proportionally to ensure you finish before your hard-stop deadline.
  • Interactive Rotary Dial: Turn the bezel or scrub the orbiting circle to fine-tune active session timing second-by-second.
  • Time Bank Protection: Finish tasks early to bank spare time in your piggy bank, automatically absorbing unexpected overruns.
  • Chronological & Sequential Modes: Work against fixed scheduled start times or flow smoothly from task to task in sequential plan mode.
  • Full Calendar Day, Week & Month Views: Visual drag-and-drop timeline schedule syncs automatically with your daily focus timer.
  • Installable PWA & Offline Ready: Works offline on iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac, and Windows with zero lag.
